Vida Rucli
takes care of Robida collective’s publications and programs.
She writes, edits and curates while living in Topolò/Topolove (IT).

Vida Rucli – architect, curator, editor – tends ruins and commons, edits publications, convenes people and drafts projects where dwelling means committing and curating is placemaking. “There is no life that is not geographic” writes Ruth Wilson Gilmore. She chose her geography: the small border village of Topolò/Topolove, where she started a collective publishing project - Robida - that later became a spatial practice, a radio signal, a garden, an orchard and an infrastructure for collective life. She cares for and holds dear the blackberry–plant of the commons, clearings in the deep forest, reading poetic theory, effervescence of minds, tender militancy.

She lectured in different universities, collaborated with the festival Climate Care (Floating University, Berlin, 2023) and is a member of the international research group Ecologies of Care and of Floating University, Berlin. Since May 2025 she is guiding, in the role of president of the board, the Institute for Slovene Culture in San Pietro al Natisone/Špietar.
